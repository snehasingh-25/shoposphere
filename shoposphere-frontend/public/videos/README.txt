Upload your hang tutorial video here as:

  hang-frame.mp4

This folder is copied to dist/videos/ on build. On Hostinger, the file must end up at:

  public_html/videos/hang-frame.mp4

Do not use paths with spaces (e.g. "Hard printed videos") — the SPA may serve index.html instead of the video.

Verify in the browser: open /videos/hang-frame.mp4 and confirm the video plays (not the shop homepage).
